# Track Titan

> Track Titan is a sim racing analytics and coaching platform (tracktitan.io) that records in-game telemetry across simulators such as iRacing, Assetto Corsa / ACC, F1 2020-26, Automobilista 2, Forza Motorsport, and Le Mans Ultimate, and turns it into lap-time analysis, AI coaching feedback, esports car setups, and structured learning content for more than 295,000 members. It is a consumer product; no public developer API, SDK, or docs surface is published as of 2026-07-21.
